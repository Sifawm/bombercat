# BomberCat v1 scope

Decisions agreed for the first version. Terms follow [CONTEXT.md](../CONTEXT.md); the reasoning behind
the two architectural choices is in [adr/](./adr/). Numbers marked *tunable* are starting values.

## Game

- **Mode:** Battle only, 2 local human Players. No campaign (ever), no AI and no online in v1.
- **Match:** best of 3 Rounds. A Round lasts at most 2:00 (*tunable*). Time running out, or both Cats
  dying in the same Explosion, is a Draw and the Round is replayed. No sudden death.
- **Round reset:** the Map is restored and all Powerups are lost.
- **Cats:** Turing, Berni, Aquiles, Hami and Ada Lovelace. Appearance only; identical stats.

## Rules

- **Movement:** continuous, 4 directions, with corner assist. Everything else occupies whole Tiles.
- **Starting stats:** Bomb Capacity 1, Range 2, base speed (*tunable*).
- **Bomb:** Fuse of 3 s (*tunable*). Blocks movement; its owner may walk off its Tile but not back on.
- **Explosion:** cross-shaped, lethal for about 0.5 s (*tunable*). Stopped by Walls; destroys the
  first Crate it reaches and stops there. Triggers Chain Reactions. Destroys Powerups on the floor.
  One hit kills.
- **Powerups:** the six listed under v1 in [powerups.md](./powerups.md).

## Map

- **Elements:** Floor, Wall (variants `brick`, `brick_low`, `pillar`), Crate, Spawn Point.
- **Only Map:** `backyard`, 13 x 11 Tiles including the border. Brick border, pillars on a
  checkerboard, 4 corner Spawn Points each with a clear L of 3 Tiles, Crates placed by hand with
  point symmetry over roughly 75 % of the free Tiles.
- **Powerup Pool of `backyard`** (*tunable*): Yarn Ball 6, Tuna Can 6, Catnip 4, Paw Swipe 2,
  Nine Lives 1, Cucumber 2.
- **Map Studio:** GridMap workspace in the Godot editor with a mesh palette, validation warnings
  (at least 2 Spawn Points, each with room to escape its first Bomb) and test-play.

## Controls

| | Keyboard | Gamepad |
|---|---|---|
| Player 1 | WASD, Space (bomb), Q (meow) | Gamepad 1: stick or d-pad, A (bomb) |
| Player 2 | Arrows, Enter (bomb), Right Shift (meow) | Gamepad 2: stick or d-pad, A (bomb) |

Fixed bindings, no remapping. Esc or Start pauses. F11 toggles fullscreen.

## Screens

Title -> Cat Select -> Round -> Round Result -> Match Result, plus Pause and How to Play.

- **Cat Select:** both Players choose at once; the same Cat cannot be picked twice; includes a
  Random option. No map selection while there is a single Map.
- **HUD:** time left and, per Player, portrait, Rounds won and Powerups held.
- **Match Result:** winner, per-Player summary (Bombs placed, Crates destroyed, Powerups collected),
  Rematch and Back to Cat Select.
- **UI language:** English, no translation system.

## Camera

Fixed, looking from the south at about 55-60 degrees, no rotation, narrow field of view, whole Map
always visible. Screen shake on Explosions; brief slow motion and zoom on the final elimination of
a Round.

## Animation

Cat skeletal animations come from Blender (`idle`, `walk`, `place_bomb`, `die`, `win`; no new ones
in v1). Everything else is done in Godot with tweens, shaders and particles:

- Bomb: pulse and red blink that speed up as the Fuse runs out; fuse spark. Slides and bounces when
  pushed.
- Explosion: grows from the centre outwards with flash, particles and smoke.
- Crate: breaks into pieces; its Powerup pops out.
- Powerup: floats up and down, spins slowly and glows; pop and rising icon when collected; brief
  flare when destroyed.
- Cat states: aura for Nine Lives and blinking while invulnerable; dizzy spiral and green tint when
  under a Curse; short trail with Catnip.
- Round: Cats drop onto their Spawn Points; "3, 2, 1, Go!"; winner banner with confetti.
- UI: screen transitions, bouncing buttons, timer pulsing in the last 10 s.

## Audio

CC0 sources only; every file is recorded with its origin and licence in `docs/credits.md`.

- **Sound effects:** place bomb, fuse tick that speeds up, explosion (stronger on a Chain Reaction),
  Crate breaking, Powerup collected, Cucumber collected, Paw Swipe, Nine Lives absorbing a hit,
  death, victory, meow, UI move/confirm/cancel, countdown and last-10-seconds warning.
- **Music:** one loop for Title and Cat Select, one for the Round that speeds up in the last 30 s.
- **Volume:** music on/off and effects on/off in the Pause menu. All Cats share the same voice.

## Technology

- Godot 4.7, GDScript with mandatory static typing, Compatibility renderer.
- Targets: Linux and Windows desktop (primary), web (secondary). Base resolution 1920 x 1080, 16:9.
- Three layers: rules (no nodes, owns movement, fixed time step), presentation, control.
- All balance values live in a single configuration resource.
- Tests cover the rules layer only, as plain scripts run with Godot headless.
- Models are generated by Blender Python scripts in `art/` and exported as GLB into the game.
- All files and code are in English and follow SOLID.

## Build order

1. **Foundations** - restructure the repository, rename assets to English, create the Godot project.
2. **Playable core** - rules layer with tests and a minimal presentation on a placeholder Map.
3. **Map Studio and `backyard`**.
4. **Powerups** - rules, Powerup Pool and the six models.
5. **Match and screens** - full flow, gamepads, How to Play.
6. **Polish** - all animation, effects, audio and extras.
7. **Export** - Linux, Windows and web builds.

## Out of scope for v1

AI Players, online multiplayer, more than 2 Players, sudden death, per-Cat abilities or voices,
special Tiles (conveyors, teleports, ice), additional Maps, options screen and key remapping,
persistent statistics, attract mode, mobile and touch controls, store distribution.
