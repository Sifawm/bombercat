# Credits

Every audio file of BomberCat, with its origin and licence. A file may only be added to
`game/assets/audio/` together with its row here.

All of them were published by their authors under
[CC0 1.0](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/) (public domain dedication), so no
attribution is required; they are credited anyway. [`art/import_audio.py`](../art/import_audio.py)
downloads each one from the origin below, evens out its volume and converts it to Ogg Vorbis;
nothing else is changed.
